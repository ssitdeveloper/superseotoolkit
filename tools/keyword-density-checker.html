Frequently Asked Questions

What is keyword density? +
Keyword density is the percentage of times a keyword appears in your content compared to the total word count. It's calculated as (keyword count / total words) × 100.
What's the ideal keyword density? +
The ideal keyword density is typically 1-2% for your primary keyword. Anything above 3% may be considered keyword stuffing, which can harm your SEO rankings.
Is this tool accurate? +
Yes! This tool accurately counts all words and keywords in your text. It filters out common stop words and provides precise density calculations.
