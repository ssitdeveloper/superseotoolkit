QR Code Generator

Generate QR codes from text, URLs, and data instantly.

What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a type of 2D barcode that can be scanned by smartphones and other devices. It can contain:

  • URLs and website links
  • Text messages
  • Contact information (vCard)
  • WiFi connection details
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Geographic coordinates

Use Cases

  • Marketing and advertising campaigns
  • Product packaging and labels
  • Event tickets and invitations
  • Restaurant menus (contactless)
  • Business cards and resumes
  • WiFi sharing

QR Code Sizes

Size Best For
Small (100x100) Business cards, small labels
Medium (200x200) Standard packaging, print
Large (500x500) Billboards, large posters
