Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode text to Base64 or decode Base64 strings instantly in your browser.

What is Base64?

Base64 is a binary-to-text encoding scheme that represents binary data using a 64-character alphabet. It's commonly used for:

  • Transmitting binary data over text-only protocols
  • Embedding images in HTML/CSS
  • Data transmission in APIs and emails
  • Simple obfuscation (not encryption)
