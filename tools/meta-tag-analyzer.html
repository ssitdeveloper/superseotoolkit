Meta Tag Analyzer

Analyze and optimize your meta tags, title tags, and meta descriptions for better SEO performance.

Meta Tags Guide

Title Tag +
Ideal Length: 50-60 characters
Purpose: Appears as the clickable headline in search results
Best Practices: Include your main keyword, be descriptive and compelling
Meta Description +
Ideal Length: 120-160 characters
Purpose: Appears as the snippet under the title in search results
Best Practices: Include keywords, be specific, include a call-to-action
H1 Tag +
Ideal Count: Exactly 1 per page
Purpose: Main heading that defines page content
Best Practices: Include main keyword, be descriptive
Open Graph Tags +
Purpose: Controls how content appears when shared on social media
Key Tags: og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url
Best Practices: Include all four for best social sharing
