Frequently Asked Questions

Are these SEO tools completely free?
Yes, every tool on Super SEO Toolkit is 100% free with no sign-up required. All processing happens locally in your browser.
Do you store my data or content?
Never. Every analysis happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Your content stays on your device.
Can I use these tools offline?
Yes! Super SEO Toolkit is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Install it once and use it offline anytime.
How accurate is the meta tag analysis?
Our analysis follows Google's official SEO guidelines and best practices from Moz, Ahrefs, and SEMrush. Character limits, recommendations, and scoring reflect current standards.
What keyboard shortcuts are available?
Press ? to open the shortcuts panel. Key shortcuts include Tab (switch tools), Enter (run analysis), Ctrl+C (copy results), and Alt+T (toggle theme).
