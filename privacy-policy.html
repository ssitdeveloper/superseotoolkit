Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Introduction

SuperSEOToolkit ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and how we use the information we collect.

2. Data Collection

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information:

  • Browser Data: Analytics data (anonymized) to understand tool usage
  • Contact Information: Name and email only when you voluntarily submit the contact form
  • Cookies: Only for theme preference (dark/light mode)

Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We DO NOT store any of your inputs to our tools
  • We DO NOT track your personal browsing behavior
  • We DO NOT sell your data to third parties
  • We DO NOT use fingerprinting or advanced tracking
  • All tool processing happens in your browser only

3. How We Use Information

Any information you provide is used solely for:

  • Responding to your contact inquiries
  • Improving our services based on aggregate usage patterns
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies:

  • Theme Cookie: Stores your light/dark mode preference
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use tracking cookies for advertising

5. Analytics

We use Google Analytics with anonymization enabled to understand:

  • Which tools are most popular
  • How users navigate our site
  • General performance metrics

This data is anonymized and aggregated. We cannot identify individual users.

6. Third-Party Services

Our site may contain:

  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements
  • Google Analytics: For usage analytics
  • Social Media Links: External links only

These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

7. Security

Your data security is important to us. We use:

  • HTTPS encryption for all communications
  • Secure hosting infrastructure
  • Regular security updates

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Clear cookies from your browser

9. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

We comply with:

  • GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@superseotoolkit.com
Contact Form

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
