What is Keyword Density and Why Does It Matter for SEO?

Learn what keyword density is, how to calculate it, optimal percentages, and how to use it effectively for search engine optimization.

What is Keyword Density?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a keyword appears on a webpage compared to the total number of words on that page. It's calculated as:

Keyword Density = (Keyword Count / Total Words) × 100

For example, if your page has 1,000 words and your target keyword appears 10 times, your keyword density would be 1%.

Optimal Keyword Density

The ideal keyword density depends on your content type and goals:

  • 0.5% - 1%: Low density, natural writing
  • 1% - 2%: Optimal range for most content
  • 2% - 3%: Higher density, still acceptable
  • 3%+: Potential keyword stuffing risk

Important: Modern SEO prioritizes user experience and natural writing over exact keyword percentages. Never sacrifice readability for keyword placement.

Best Practices

  • Focus on Natural Writing: Write first, optimize second
  • Use LSI Keywords: Include related keywords and synonyms
  • Place Keywords Strategically: Title, headings, first 100 words
  • Avoid Keyword Stuffing: Never force keywords unnaturally
  • Monitor Your Density: Use our tools to check regularly
  • Consider Context: Search intent matters more than density
